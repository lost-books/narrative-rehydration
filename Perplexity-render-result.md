# Render

## SHORT

In the morning, Esmeralda, the girl’s mother, gave the basket of food to Maria, the girl in the red hood. Esmeralda warned Maria, “Stay on the path.” In the dark woods, Maria met Grumple, the hungry wolf. Grumple asked Maria, “Where are you going?” Maria answered Grumple, “To grandmother’s cottage.” At grandmother’s cottage, Grumple swallowed Monique, the sick grandmother. At the bed, Grumple disguised himself as Monique. In the afternoon at grandmother’s cottage, Maria said, “What big eyes you have.” At grandmother’s cottage, Gary, the passing woodcutter, cut Grumple open. Monique, alive, emerged.

## LONG

In the morning, Esmeralda, the girl’s mother, gave the basket of food to Maria, the girl in the red hood. The basket was meant for Maria to carry onward, its contents held within it as a thing passed from mother to daughter. [inference: Maria carries a basket to her sick grandmother; Esmeralda sends Maria with the basket]

Esmeralda warned Maria, “Stay on the path.” The warning was directed at Maria before she left, fixing the path as the route she was to keep. [bridge: before Maria set out]

In the dark woods, Maria met Grumple, the hungry wolf. The woods formed the place of the meeting, and the encounter brought the girl and the wolf face to face. [texture: the woods were dark]

Grumple asked Maria, “Where are you going?” The question came from the wolf to the girl during their meeting. [bridge: during the meeting]

Maria answered Grumple, “To grandmother’s cottage.” Her answer named her destination and was aimed at the wolf. [bridge: in reply]

At grandmother’s cottage, Grumple swallowed Monique, the sick grandmother. The cottage was where the wolf reached the grandmother, and the swallowing left Monique inside the wolf. [inference: Monique lives alone in the cottage; the wolf reaches the cottage first and swallows the grandmother]

At the bed, Grumple disguised himself as Monique. The bed was the place where the wolf took on the grandmother’s appearance while waiting. [inference: the wolf waits disguised in her bed]

In the afternoon at grandmother’s cottage, Maria said, “What big eyes you have.” The words were spoken by Maria at the cottage after the wolf had taken the grandmother’s place. [bridge: when Maria arrived]

At grandmother’s cottage, Gary, the passing woodcutter, cut Grumple open. The cutting opened the wolf at the place where Maria had found the disguised figure. [inference: Gary intervenes and cuts the wolf open]

Monique, alive, emerged. Her emergence followed the wolf being cut open, and it restored her from being swallowed. [bridge: afterward]
