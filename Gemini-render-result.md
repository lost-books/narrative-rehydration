# 3. LONG
In the morning, Esmeralda, the girl's mother, handed over the basket of food to Maria, the girl in the red hood. As Maria prepared to leave, Esmeralda warned her directly, "Stay on the path."

Walking through the dark woods, Maria met Grumple, the hungry wolf. Standing along the shadowed trail, Grumple asked her, "Where are you going?" Maria answered him calmly, "To grandmother's cottage."

Afterward, Grumple reached grandmother's cottage and swiftly swallowed Monique, the sick grandmother. Settling into the bed, Grumple carefully disguised himself as Monique.

In the afternoon at grandmother's cottage, Maria entered and said, "What big eyes you have." Hearing the commotion, Gary, the passing woodcutter, rushed into grandmother's cottage and slice-cut Grumple open. From within, Monique emerged alive and well.
